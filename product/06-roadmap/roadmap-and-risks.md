# Roadmap, Metrics and Risks

## Phases
| Phase | Timing | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Prototype loop | Now | Booking, payment, manual WhatsApp dispatch, AI album; run 5–10 real events; collect testimonials |
| 2 MVP app | Weeks 3–6 | Capturer app with guided shot list and auto-upload; AI picks best moments and builds the album |
| 3 Scale | Weeks 7–12 | Partner portal, automatic assignment, spend on proven creatives, native partner app |
| Later | | Photo-based reels, live-tethered Pro mode, on-device privacy tier, raw-video highlights |

## Next-up backlog (ordered)
1. `booking_id`-scoped capture link per assignment.
2. Album push-back plus WhatsApp auto-delivery (needs approved template).
3. Retention/deletion job for raw photos.
4. Camera/Pro import screen with progressive import.
5. Pricing decision (flat fee vs % commission).
6. Trust and safety: partner verification, ratings, refund guarantee.

## North-star and supporting metrics
- **North star:** completed sessions per week with host rating ≥ 4.
- Supporting: booking conversion, partner accept rate and time-to-arrive, session-end-to-album time, repeat-booking rate, partner 30-day retention, effective partner ₹/hr.

## Risk register
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customers reject semi-pro at ₹500/hr | Medium | Fatal | Validation plan A1 before scaling |
| Partner pay too low → amateur-supply spiral | High at current pricing | High | Move to % model and higher price band |
| Cold start: no partners in market | High | High | Recruit decorators and organisers; start with own network |
| Quality inconsistency erodes trust | Medium | High | Guarantee, ratings, AI safety net |
| Privacy claim overreach / DPDP exposure | Medium | High | Honest claims, retention job, in-event notice, legal review |
| Compute cost at concurrent live sessions | Low now | Medium | Cap live concurrency; GPU tier later |
| Competitor (e.g. Clixo) validates faster dispatch | Low-Medium | Medium | Lead on delivery speed, not dispatch alone |
