# Business Model & Unit Economics

## Model
Two-sided marketplace plus proprietary AI capture software. Hosts book and pay on the website; partners (photographers, decorators, small event organisers) fulfil through the SnapAI app. Payment-first: a booking confirms only after payment.

## Partner tiers
| Tier | Equipment | Capture path |
|---|---|---|
| Snap Mobile | Partner's phone | Live AI auto-capture plus manual shutter |
| Snap Camera | Standalone camera | Shoot normally, batch-import afterward |
| Snap Pro | Own DSLR/mirrorless | Batch-import (v1); live tethering is a v2 premium feature |

## Live pricing (locked 27 Sep 2026)
| Duration | Customer price | Partner fee |
|---|---|---|
| 30 min | ₹99 | flat ₹9 / booking |
| 1 h | ₹199 | flat ₹9 |
| 2 h | ₹299 | flat ₹9 |
| 3 h | ₹399 | flat ₹9 |
| 4 h+ | ~₹99/h | flat ₹9 |

**Finding: not sustainable.** Modelled partner effective pay (after travel time and cost) ≈ ₹20/hr on the 30-min tier and ~₹56/hr on 1 h. Too low to retain reliable partners, and it reproduces the amateur-supply quality spiral seen on Snappr.

## Revised model analysed (recommended, not adopted)
| Duration | Price |
|---|---|
| 30 min | ₹299 |
| 1 h | ₹500 |
| 2 h | ₹900 |
| 3 h | ₹1,300 |

At **15% commission**: partner effective pay ≈ ₹150/hr; platform margin ≈ ₹59–73 per booking after payment, messaging and AI costs. ₹500/hr sits in the junior-professional band (~₹200–600+/hr), so it is a fair rate with a speed wrapper, not a race to the bottom.

## Why not just hire a professional?
The honest differentiator is **speed and low commitment**, not price: no multi-hour minimum, no advance booking, album within the session. If customers feel the skill gap strongly, speed alone may not win. That is the assumption to test.
