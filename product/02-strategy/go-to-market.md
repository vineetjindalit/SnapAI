# Go-To-Market Plan

**Principle:** Delhi NCR first; no real ad spend before the loop is proven.

| Phase | Name | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Internal | 5–10 free bookings with friends and family; prove the loop |
| 02 | Alpha | Real booking live, announced beyond friends |
| 03 | Beta | Meta ads on, low budget, 5–10 km radius |
| 04 | Early access | Wider radius; publish real before/after photos |
| 05 | Full launch | Self-serve booking across NCR, full ad ramp |

A simple/lovable/complete gate is checked before each phase graduates.

**Channels:** *Owned* (app, website, Instagram, WhatsApp); *rented* (Meta Reels ads, local Facebook groups and societies); *borrowed* (cross-promotion with decorators and local vendors).

**Messaging (from competitor gaps):** short sessions, no multi-hour minimum, phone-only AI capture, minutes-level delivery. Words to use: candid, on-demand, fast, affordable. Words to avoid: "professional photographer", "DSLR", "photoshoot".

**Supply side:** the cold-start risk is partners, not demand. Zero partners were registered at last check. Recruit decorators and small organisers already visiting homes; they add capture to a visit they were making anyway.

**Proof still needed:** testimonials, real before/after albums, repeat-booking rate: all zero until real events run.
