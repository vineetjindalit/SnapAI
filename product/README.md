# SnapAI — Product Management Workspace

Everything behind the product, not just the code: discovery, strategy, requirements, validation, prototyping and roadmap. Written as a working PM record — assumptions are labelled, estimates are labelled, and unknowns are listed as unknowns.

| Stage | Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | `01-discovery/` | Market research, competitor analysis, Delhi NCR market sizing |
| 2. Strategy | `02-strategy/` | Business model, unit economics, go-to-market plan |
| 3. Requirements | `03-requirements/` | PRD, partner-capture system design |
| 4. Validation | `04-validation/` | Riskiest-assumption tests, interview guide, success criteria |
| 5. Prototyping | `05-prototyping/` | What is built, what was verified, what was not |
| 6. Roadmap | `06-roadmap/` | Phased roadmap, metrics, risk register |

**Product in one line:** book a nearby "capturer" in minutes for a short event; an AI engine auto-captures the key moments, culls and enhances them, and delivers an album on WhatsApp.

**Status (Oct 2026):** pre-launch. Booking and payment flow live; AI engine running; no real partner-fulfilled events yet. Key open risk: will customers accept a semi-pro capturer at a junior-professional price? See `04-validation/`.

> Numbers marked *(estimate)* are reasoned, not measured. Numbers marked *(measured)* come from actual runs of the engine.
