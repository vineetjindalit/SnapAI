# Delhi NCR Market Sizing

**Honest headline:** there is no public dataset splitting Delhi NCR photography demand by use case. Real anchors are cited; splits are labelled estimates.

## Real anchors
| Metric | Figure |
|---|---|
| India wedding photography market (2026) | ~USD 5.18B |
| India wedding services market (2025) | USD 32.98B, 12.16% CAGR to 2034 |
| India influencer marketing (2026) | ~₹3,375 crore, ~25%/yr growth |
| Delhi ASI monument visitors, FY24-25 | 7,950,048 (Qutub Minar 3.44M, Red Fort 2.96M, Humayun's Tomb 1.16M) |

## Willingness-to-pay ceiling (published India rates)
Beginner ₹0–2,500/hr; amateur ₹1,250–5,000/hr; professional ₹5,000–15,000/hr; Delhi NCR metro ≈ ₹4,000/hr. A ₹500/hr SnapAI session sits below the amateur band: aimed at "can't or won't book a real photographer for one hour".

## TAM / SAM / SOM sketch
- **TAM (India):** ≥ USD 5B/yr wedding photography alone, plus uncounted casual photography.
- **SAM (Delhi NCR):** estimated 4–6% of national non-wedding casual demand.
- **SOM (Year 1–2):** a few thousand paid sessions per year, not hundreds of thousands.
