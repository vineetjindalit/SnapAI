## Delhi NCR: local full-service (ad-verified)
Hora, Pavitr Chitr (₹2,999–4,999 for 3–4 h), hunnybunny_photography. All sell hours-long coverage; none has time-tiered pricing; none messages "AI-assisted" or "no DSLR"; none addresses short events. Their ads ran ~40 days unchanged, a low-experimentation category.

## Global precedent
| Platform | Model | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Snappr | Hourly tiers, 20–35% commission, 48–72 h delivery, $42M raised | Scaled by diversifying beyond commission; quality complaints grow with volume |
| Flytographer | Fixed curated packages | Curated beats open at premium |
| Thumbtack | Pay-per-lead $10–100+ | Suppliers resent paying for unconverted leads |
| Kodakit | "Uber of photography" | Died after supplier revolt over contract terms |

## Gaps SnapAI can own
- **Messaging:** "you don't need 4 hours for a 30-minute cake cutting."
- **Speed:** nobody claims minutes-level album delivery.
- **Privacy:** nobody claims a no-human-review pipeline (state carefully; see PRD §Privacy).
- **Weakness to close:** established photographers win on "verified professional" trust. Needs a guarantee and visible ratings.

Lensroom and most booking-software tools solve discovery and scheduling, not the production problem of turning 300–500 raw shots into a curated album.
