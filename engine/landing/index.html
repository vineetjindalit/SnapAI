Real-time moment detection

Real CLIP + custom-trained classifiers identify ceremonial peaks in under 80 ms per frame. 11 default moment types, expandable per event.

😀

Emotion-aware capture

AffectNet-trained FER recognises joy, surprise, and intimacy. Combined with iris-based gaze (L2CS-Net), SnapAI waits for the right moment, not just any moment.

🧠

Learns your style

Click 👍 or 👎 on captures. Per-event Bayesian priors plus CLIP "kept-frame centroids" mean the model gets better at YOUR aesthetic over the course of one event.

🎙

Voice + text live prompts

Switch what to capture mid-event by typing or speaking — "also catch confetti" works exactly like you'd say it. No menu diving.

⚡

Runs on your laptop

Apple Silicon MPS, NVIDIA CUDA, or pure CPU — no cloud lock-in. Tiered model zoo: install only what you want from MediaPipe → YOLO → CLIP.

Photographer-in-the-loop

Review and curate. Drag to reorder. Share a magic link. You always own the photos and the choice. The AI is your assistant, not your replacement.
