Terms

Last updated: 26 September 2026

These terms apply when you book a SnapAI capturer or register as a SnapAI partner.

The service

SnapAI provides event capture only: a capturer attends your event for the booked duration (30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 3 hours, or a custom number of hours you choose) and captures moments using the SnapAI app, including AI auto-capture and manual shots. SnapAI does not provide decoration, catering or event planning.

Bookings and payment

A booking is confirmed only once payment is completed via Razorpay on the booking page. Prices are shown in Indian Rupees; for a custom number of hours, the price is calculated at ₹99 per hour.

Our promise and refunds

If your capturer does not arrive, or the capture fails, you get a full refund. If you cancel more than 24 hours before the event, you get a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours may not be refunded.

Album delivery

Your private album is delivered instantly: as soon as your event ends, your capturer generates it on the SnapAI app and shares it straight to your WhatsApp.

Partners

Partners are independent service providers, not employees. Payout per event is agreed on the verification call, minus a flat ₹9 platform access fee charged per booking fulfilled. Partners must follow SnapAI's conduct guidelines, reach events on time and keep customer photos private. Full partner terms are shared on WhatsApp when a partner registers.
