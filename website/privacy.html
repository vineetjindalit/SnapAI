Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

SnapAI ("we") provides event capture services in Delhi NCR, India. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you book or register as a partner we collect your name, mobile number, optional email, event details (type, date, time, area, guest count), your chosen plan, language preference and how you found us. Partners also share their partner type, experience, areas served, phone model and an optional portfolio link.

Calls and WhatsApp

With your consent (the tick box on our forms) we contact you by phone call, including AI-assisted calls, and by WhatsApp about your booking or partnership. You can ask us to stop at any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp or telling us on the call.

Your event photos

Photos and videos captured at your event are private. They are shared only with you through a private album link. We do not use your event photos in our marketing unless you give separate written permission. Albums are deleted from our systems 90 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them longer.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment partner. We do not store your card or bank details.

Who we share data with

Only with the service providers we need to run SnapAI (hosting, database, payments, calling and messaging) and with the capturer assigned to your event, who receives only the details needed to reach your event.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 83684 92368.
